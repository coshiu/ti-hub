Partnership Framework

A working agreement and training guide defining how Recruiters and Sourcers partner to deliver world-class hiring outcomes.

Sourcing and recruiting operate as equal strategic partners. Sourcing drives market intelligence, discovery, and early-stage strategy, while recruiting leads stakeholder alignment, candidate experience, and closing. Together, this partnership is the engine behind our hiring strategy — translating business goals into world-class hires.

Primary Ownership
Who Owns What

Built on three principles: One Team, Complementary Strengths, and a Continuous Feedback Loop.

Sourcer — Primary Ownership
→
Calibration & Intake Partner with recruiter on detailed calibration sessions. Define archetypes, green/red flags, and must-haves. Maintain a real-time feedback journal tagged by theme (e.g. too senior, too infra heavy, not enough scale experience).
→
Top-of-Funnel (ToF) Own outreach strategy, multi-touch sequences, and pipeline generation. Conduct candidate pre-screens to improve calibration and advocate for qualified candidates. If ToF is low, prioritize identification and delegate screens to recruiters until healthy.
→
Pipeline Health Ensure consistent candidate flow. Rebalance profiles as searches evolve. Flag risks early. Deep dive into bottlenecks — pass-through rates, interviewer feedback, candidate feedback.
→
Market Data & Industry Trends Provide data on talent availability, competitor activity, compensation trends, and overall industry trends.
Recruiters are encouraged to source outbound candidates, especially when support is needed.
Recruiter — Primary Ownership
→
Intake Meetings Schedule and lead intake with the hiring manager. Clarify scope, urgency, leveling, compensation, and interview plan including panel participants.
→
Job Opening & External Posting Gather final job details, finalize JD, review posting language, and ensure all systems (e.g. Ashby) are correctly set up before opening.
→
Role Kickoff & Stakeholder Management Lead kickoff meetings with HMs and hiring team. Own ongoing communication and alignment with all interviewers.
→
Applicant Review & Offer / Closing Manage inbound applications, referrals, and internal mobility. Drive offer strategy, compensation alignment, and candidate closing.
Sourcers are encouraged to assist with inbound applicants, especially when support is needed.
Mandatory Agreements
Service Level Agreements

These SLAs are mandatory for all roles. Exceptions for niche or specialized roles are noted where applicable.

New Sourced Lead
Send an initial outreach/engagement message within 48 hours of identification.
Exceptions can be made for niche/specialized roles with unique engagement strategies.
Both 48 hrs
Qualified Lead Confirmation
Move the engaged, qualified candidate to the appropriate next stage within 24 hours of their confirmation of interest.
Both 24 hrs
Active Candidates — Stage Timeline
Ensure candidates are moving through appropriate interview stages within 48 hours. Send "no update, updates" for special cases to keep candidates informed.
Both 48 hrs
Urgent Need for Talent
Proactively inform Sourcer via direct message of the urgent need and specific profile required to immediately re-prioritize sourcing efforts.
Recruiter Immediate
Interview Feedback Submission
All interviewers must submit feedback into ATS within 24–48 hours of interview completion. Both Recruiter and Sourcer hold interviewers accountable.
Both 24–48 hrs
Unresponsive Sourced Candidate
Sourcer sends follow-up after 5 business days. If still no response, partner with Recruiter to provide context and brainstorm an appropriate re-engagement strategy.
Sourcer 5 bus. days
Pipeline Stall — Ad-hoc Sync
Hold an urgent ad-hoc sync upon identifying a stall. Use Slack to keep alignment flowing if HM meeting window can't be met within the suggested timeframe.
Both 24–48 hrs
Operating Principles
Philosophy & Culture

The beliefs that govern how we work — individually and together.

Sourcing Philosophy
Strategy first,
search second.
◆We map before we message.
◆We calibrate before we chase.
◆We influence with data, not assumptions.
Recruiting Philosophy
Precision first,
process second.
◆We align before we advance.
◆We communicate before we assume.
◆We convert insights into outcomes.
What Good Partnership Looks Like
✓Sourcer brings clarity; recruiter brings alignment.
✓Over-communication protects the candidate's experience.
✓Feedback and data flow both ways.
✓Misalignments are addressed in real time.
✓Every intake results in artifacts: market map, outreach plan, candidate archetypes, green/red flags, calibration summary.
✓Clean handoffs reduce confusion, duplicate effort, and ensure a positive employer brand reputation.

Sourcers and recruiters co-lead a hiring engine built on precision, data, and partnership. When both functions operate in sync, we move faster, hire smarter, and deliver a world-class experience for every candidate and hiring team.
