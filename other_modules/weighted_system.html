This was built to add a layer of context to how we measure and deploy recruiting capacity. A consistent hiring target is the right foundation, and understanding the complexity behind each req is what makes that target meaningful. Closing a Principal Systems Engineer and closing a Senior Backend SWE require fundamentally different strategies, timelines, and levels of market expertise. Strategic sourcing delivers the most value on niche, specialized roles precisely because those searches demand a completely different approach: deeper market mapping, longer runway, tighter calibration, and the kind of candidate relationships that don't come from inbound volume alone. TalentIQ makes that complexity visible so sourcing support goes where it creates the most leverage.

Speed

Knowing immediately which reqs need sourcing support, and who is best positioned to own them, eliminates a layer of coordination overhead that compounds across every open req.

Efficiency

Portfolio-adjusted goals let every recruiter and sourcer be measured fairly against the actual complexity of their work. Good performance means something specific here, not just a number hit.

Quality

Pairing sourcers to reqs by domain expertise and existing calibration means we're not starting from zero on every search. Relationships and institutional knowledge compound over time.

Welcome to TalentIQ

A weighted performance system that replaces flat hire targets with a model calibrated to actual role complexity. Recruiter north star: offer accepts. Sourcer north star: offer extends from sourcer-originated pipeline (first touch + Ashby “sourced” tag). Recruiter and sourcer quarterly target: 9 weighted points — editable per person on the Progress tab.

Step 1
Score roles
Enter a role title and department, paste the JD, and click Auto-score. Claude scores 5 complexity dimensions and applies team floors and role overrides automatically. Bulk upload via CSV is also supported.
Step 2
Progress
Log offer accepts (recruiters) and offer extends (sourcers). Weighted points update in real time per person. Edit, rename, or remove any entry or person. Bulk upload via CSV supported. Quarterly target is editable on each card.
Step 3
Diagnose
Log weekly funnel data per person and req. PTR at each stage (outreach → HM screen → technical → onsite → offer) is compared to tier-adjusted targets and flagged green, amber, or red. Coaching signal only — not scored.
Step 4
Reference
Full model documentation: point values by tier, team-level complexity floors, role-level overrides, PTR targets, weekly outreach targets for sourcers, and stalled search buffer rules.

Tier model & point values

Tier 1 Score 5–10 · Broad pool · Rec 1.0 pt · Src 1.5 pts
Tier 2 Score 11–15 · Domain specific · Rec 1.5 pts · Src 2.0 pts
Tier 3 Score 16–20 · Scarce talent · Rec 2.5 pts · Src 3.5 pts
Tier 4 Score 21–25 · Sub-100 candidates · Rec 4.0 pts · Src 5.0 pts
Recruiter and sourcer point values differ because sourcers influence the outcome earlier in the funnel and across more reqs simultaneously.

Key rules

· Quarterly target: 9 pts for both recruiters and sourcers (editable per person)
· Recruiters: 9 pts = 3 hires/month at Tier 1. Higher-tier closes count proportionally more
· Sourcers: credit applies to offer extends from sourcer-originated pipeline only
· Attribution: first touch + Ashby “sourced” tag. Credit follows the candidate regardless of team or recruiter change
· Stall buffer: 0.5 pts per stalled req outside TA control, capped at 1.0 pts/qtr. Requires a logged reason code
· Diagnostics (Step 3) are coaching signals only — they do not affect point totals
· Team floors: Foundations ≥16 · Network Eng & Managed AI ≥13 · All other Cloud Eng ≥11
All data saves automatically to your browser. Export CSV on any tab to share with your team. Use “Clear all progress data” on the Progress tab to reset.
