Sourcing and recruiting operate as equal strategic partners. Full engagement model lives in the Recruiter / Sourcer Partnership Framework.

01 Operating Structure
This model organizes sourcing across technical domains with clear primary ownership, complexity-weighted resource allocation, and a controlled flex layer. Unlike ad hoc or evenly distributed models, it concentrates effort where it drives the highest hiring impact — reducing search stall risk, improving pipeline quality, and preserving continuity at every stage.

03 Outcomes
01
Faster time-to-pipeline
Domain depth reduces ramp-up. Sourcers already know communities, titles, and signals.
No cold starts on every new req.
02
Risk-weighted effort
Hard-to-fill, high-stakes roles receive proportionally more investment — not equal splits.
Scarce talent gets the attention it deserves.
03
Continuity & trust
Consistent ownership builds HM and candidate relationships that improve offer acceptance.
Fewer re-starts. Stronger close rates.
04
Scalable without chaos
Flex expands coverage during spikes without breaking accountability or creating ambiguity.
Hiring surges don't break the system.

Operating Standards

Internal operating targets and model parameters effective for the current sourcing structure.

The sourcing model is shifting to a defined-role, complexity-aligned structure. Sourcers now have clearly defined roles assigned to them, moving away from the previous model where sourcers were broadly aligned to an org.
P4+
Scope: P4 & Above + Leadership

Sourcing support allocated to P4+ and all leadership levels. Exception: Foundations org includes P3 sourcing support given the niche technical depth of those roles.

10 min
Minimum 10 Roles per Sourcer

Each sourcer carries a minimum of 10 active roles at any time. This ensures full utilization and focused, quality execution across the portfolio.

→
Defined Role Assignments

Sourcers now have clearly defined roles assigned to them, moving away from the previous model where sourcers were broadly aligned to an org.

3 / mo
Goal: 3 Offer Extends / Month

Each sourcer is goaled at 3 offer extends per month — an average target reflecting balanced allocation across complexity tiers.

80 / wk
Outreach Goal: 80 / Week

Baseline outreach target is 80 per week per sourcer. Subject to change by role complexity — higher-complexity roles may require fewer, higher-quality touches.

≡ form
Request Form for Sourcing Support

Recruiters or HMs requesting support outside defined scope may submit a request form. Allocations reviewed and approved by leadership.
