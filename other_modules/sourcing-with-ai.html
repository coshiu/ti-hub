Stage 01 of 07Intake & Role Understanding

Intake & Role Understanding

Goal: Get technical clarity fast so you can calibrate with confidence.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Summarize this job description into 3 buckets: core systems knowledge, domain-specific expertise, and differentiators between senior and staff-level talent."
✓ Shared language pre-search ✓ Clear must-haves vs. nice-to-haves ✓ Faster calibration cycles
How to use AI
Blueprint Creation: Summarize intake notes into a role blueprint covering scope, level, must-haves, and differentiators.
Skill Decomposition: Break down key skill clusters (e.g., "What are core subdomains of storage engineering?").
Market Alignment: Compare internal descriptions vs. market equivalents to align terminology.
Best Practice: Always verify AI-generated blueprints with your HM before sourcing starts — AI may miss org-specific context.
Stage 02 of 07Market Mapping

Market Mapping

Goal: Identify where qualified talent actually sits — faster and cleaner.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"List companies known for developing hypervisor or kernel-level technology in the AI/Cloud Infra space."
✓ Strategic market maps ✓ Higher hit rates ✓ Hidden talent pools
How to use AI
Target Generation: Generate lists of target companies by tech stack, product type, or infrastructure model.
Org Classification: Classify orgs by technical profile (e.g., "Which companies have built distributed file systems?").
Signal Detection: Cross-check public profiles for emerging players or acquisition spin-offs.
Best Practice: AI-generated company lists are a starting point. Cross-reference with LinkedIn or Crunchbase before building outreach lists.
Stage 03 of 07Search String Design

Search String Design

Goal: Build smarter boolean strings and avoid keyword bias in your sourcing.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Create a boolean string for senior engineers experienced in hypervisor or virtualization stack development (KVM, Xen, QEMU, VMware). Exclude front-end and QA roles."
✓ Cleaner pipelines ✓ Higher signal-to-noise ✓ Broader title coverage
How to use AI
Generate Variations: Create complex boolean variations for niche roles automatically.
Synonym Detection: Spot synonyms, alternate titles, and jargon you might miss.
Pre-Testing: Test strings for false positives before running in LinkedIn or SeekOut.
Best Practice: High volume doesn't mean high signal. Sample 10–15 profiles after deploying any AI-built string before scaling.
Stage 04 of 07Calibration

Calibration & Pattern Recognition

Goal: Strengthen judgment through comparison, not volume.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Summarize the common experience patterns among these three storage engineers that indicate readiness for staff-level scope."
✓ Faster calibration loops ✓ Less second-guessing ✓ Increased HM trust
How to use AI
Profile Comparison: Compare two profiles side-by-side; ask AI to summarize relative strengths or fit.
Pattern Extraction: Extract shared patterns across high-signal candidates (company lineage, keywords, project scope).
Archetype Creation: Turn calibration notes into archetype profiles for the hiring team.
Best Practice: Feed 3–5 confirmed strong profiles — not maybes. Input quality determines archetype quality.
Stage 05 of 07Outreach Craft

Outreach Craft

Goal: Write high-conversion outreach faster without sounding generic.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Write a concise, authentic outreach message to a Staff Virtualization Engineer at Google who's worked on KVM, highlighting impact, ownership, and our AI infrastructure mission."
✓ Handcrafted feel at scale ✓ Higher reply rates ✓ Consistent team voice
How to use AI
Tone A/B Testing: Draft multiple variants — technical, conversational, value-driven — and test.
Relevance Mirroring: Mirror language from candidate profiles (project names, tech stacks) for immediate relevance.
Asset Creation: Build a shared message bank for instant reuse across similar roles.
Data Hygiene: Never paste candidate names, contact info, or identifying details into a public AI model. Use role context only.
Stage 06 of 07Pipeline Management

Pipeline Management & Reporting

Goal: Spend less time formatting updates, more time sourcing.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Summarize this pipeline data into a brief update for leadership: outcomes, risks, and next moves."
✓ Instant leadership clarity ✓ Faster root-cause detection ✓ More time for sourcing
How to use AI
Snapshot Creation: Summarize weekly pipeline movement into status snapshots for HMs or execs.
Funnel Analysis: Analyze PTRs or stage-drop reasons to spot systemic issues.
Action Planning: Generate follow-up plans for stalled candidates.
Best Practice: Validate AI-summarized numbers against Ashby before sharing with leadership. AI is only as accurate as your inputs.
Stage 07 of 07Reflection & Learning

Reflection & Continuous Learning

Goal: Treat AI as a sourcing partner, not a shortcut.

⟩_ Example Prompt
"Suggest 3 alternative sourcing strategies for a search that has stalled for senior-level SDN engineers."
✓ Faster strategy iteration ✓ Sharper domain fluency ✓ Better cycle refresh
How to use AI
Search Debriefs: Ask "What did I miss?" after a tough search to surface blind spots.
Pivot Strategy: Request alternative sourcing angles after a failed search cycle.
Technical Fluency: Keep learning through guided Q&A (e.g., "Explain I/O virtualization").
Best Practice: Save your best prompts to a shared team doc after each sprint. What works for one search often transfers to the next.
